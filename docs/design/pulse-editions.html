Christmas Edition — rules

  • Concept: a holiday issue of a broadsheet. Light mode inverts the masthead: evergreen masthead and hero on a cool white page, white nav, burgundy for the breaking bar and actions, gold only as fine rules and ornaments. Dark mode keeps the masthead on the dark page. The one memorable device is the sprig-flanked hero with a double gold keyline.
  • Cards: radius 2px, 1px border --line, 4px double --rule top edge. Section header: eyebrow, Playfair 900 uppercase, 1px rule with a centered holly mark. Nav: white bar in light mode (near-black in dark), 2px gold underline. Breaking bar: burgundy. Buttons: burgundy (light) or gold (dark), radius 2px.
  • Placement: sprig corners in the masthead (desktop only, hidden under 900px); holly divider on homepage section headers only; edition stamp under the dateline; hero between the breaking bar and content, homepage only. Not used on article pages, cards, forms, the Owners Panel or the footer. Mobile hero drops the sprigs and keeps the keylines and headline.
  • Optional switches: hero banner, ornaments (sprigs and holly), edition stamp, snowfall. With all four off, the evergreen/burgundy/gold palette, double-rule cards and gold-underlined nav still read as a Christmas edition.

Sports Edition — rules

  • Concept: the sports section of a serious paper. Navy and clay with white lane lines. One extra font: Barlow Condensed 800 italic, used only for headlines and the bib label; body stays Source Serif 4. The memorable device is the hero: curved lane lines, lane numbers 1 to 4, a checkered finish line, and the headline on a skewed navy slab.
  • Cards: radius 0, 4px clay left bar. Section header: double-chevron eyebrow mark, Barlow italic uppercase, 4px ink rule with a 64px clay lead segment. Nav: white bar in light mode (deep navy in dark), 4px clay underline. Breaking bar: navy in light mode, clay in dark. Masthead: navy in light mode, so the clay hero is the only large clay block. Buttons: clay, radius 0. Label: white race-bib chip with two pin dots.
  • Placement: the track appears only in the hero. Chevron mark and clay rule segment only on homepage section headers. Not used behind sections, on article pages, forms, the Owners Panel or the footer. Mobile hero swaps to a dedicated 400×84 track SVG.
  • Optional switches: hero banner, ornaments (chevrons and clay segment), bib label, speed streak. With all four off, the navy/clay palette, italic condensed headlines, square cards and clay-underlined nav remain.

Both editions

  • Photo slot (optional): hero 1200×140 desktop and 400×84 mobile (about 8.6:1 and 4.8:1), focal point centered, covered by a 55% --hero-bg overlay so hero text stays at 4.5:1. The edition works without a photo.
  • Motion: Christmas: 40 small flakes (22 on mobile) drift down over the utility bar and masthead only and fade out before the nav, so the breaking bar, hero banner and content are never touched. Flakes are 2 to 4.5px, 50 to 90% opacity, 9 to 18s per fall, pointer-events none. Sports: one soft white streak sweeps the hero lanes every 7s. Both are removed under prefers-reduced-motion and by the Motion effect switch.
  • Logo: the crest is a placeholder here. Use the real file, never recolored.
  • Fonts: Playfair Display and Source Serif 4 as on the live site; Barlow Condensed (Sports only) is the single addition. Load it only when Sports is active.
